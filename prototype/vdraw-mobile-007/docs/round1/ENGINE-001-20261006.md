## Behavior

External JSON/import, Editor construction/update, storage writes and checksum-valid storage restoration already call `validate`. Its arc contract now rejects non-finite or nonnumeric angles, absolute angles above 360,000,000 degrees (one million turns), spans above that same supported limit, mismatched/zero/negative radii and overflowing coordinate extents. Rejection does not modify the submitted document, accepted document, Undo history or existing saved record. This explicit supported range rejects the reported `1e308` and `1e20` cases and avoids silently normalizing corrupt originals.

`arcPoints` independently applies the arc contract, returning no points for invalid raw objects. For valid arcs, negative/zero spans normalize by modulo in constant time rather than repeated addition; positive spans retain multiple turns. Stored angles are not rewritten. Normal arcs still produce exactly 49 points. `render.shape` omits invalid/non-finite arc polylines, selection omits invalid arcs, and hit testing cannot match an invalid arc.

Ordinary positive/negative angles, wraparound, equal angles, full circles and explicit 720-degree arcs were compared with the pre-fix point formula. All checked coordinates agree within 1e-9. No change to storage schema, history codec, page/element IDs or non-arc validation.
